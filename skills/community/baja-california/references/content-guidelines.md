# Content Guidelines

## Language Policy
- All content MUST be bilingual (English + Spanish)
- Use the `nameEs`/`titleEs`/`descriptionEs` fields for Spanish versions
- For government terms, use the official Spanish term first with English in parentheses
  - Example: "Credencial para Votar (INE Voter ID)"
  - Example: "Clave Unica de Registro de Poblacion (CURP)"

## Tone & Voice
- **Warm and community-focused**: Write like a helpful neighbor, not a government website
- **Encouraging**: "You can do this!" not "You must comply with..."
- **Practical**: Focus on what people actually need to do, step by step
- **Inclusive**: This serves everyone — Mexican nationals, expats, visitors, all ages

## Accuracy Standards
- Only include verified information from official government sources
- Include direct links to official websites (gob.mx, sat.gob.mx, etc.)
- Flag any information that may change frequently with a note: "Verify current requirements at [official site]"
- Include last-verified dates on resources

## Content Rules
- Never include personal opinions about government efficiency
- Never recommend specific professionals over others without clear criteria
- Always include multiple contact methods when available
- Respect privacy — only list publicly available contact information
- Do NOT include pricing for professional services (prices change frequently)

## Categories

### Charity Categories
- `orphanage` — Children's homes and support organizations
- `shelter` — Homeless shelters, women's shelters, disaster relief
- `food-bank` — Food distribution, comedores comunitarios
- `medical` — Free clinics, medicine distribution, health campaigns
- `infrastructure` — Road repair, community building, public space improvement
- `education` — Schools, tutoring, scholarship programs
- `other` — Other charitable activities

### Professional Categories
- `lawyer` — Specialties: immigration, real estate, business, criminal, family
- `doctor` — General practitioners and specialists
- `dentist` — Dental care providers
- `accountant` — Tax advisors, bookkeepers, financial consultants
- `real-estate` — Real estate agents and property managers
- `translator` — Interpreters and document translators
- `notary` — Notarios publicos (legal notaries)
- `other` — Other professional services

### Resource Categories
- `tramites` — General government procedures
- `passport` — Mexican passport (new/renewal)
- `id-ine` — INE voter ID card
- `curp` — CURP registration/correction
- `rfc` — RFC tax ID registration
- `birth-certificate` — Acta de nacimiento
- `residency` — Temporary/permanent resident cards
- `immigration` — Immigration procedures, visas, work permits
- `general` — Other resources
